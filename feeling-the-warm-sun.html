Descriptions of Things I Love the Most

Feeling the Warm Sun

Out of the cold water or out of a dark house. When I haven’t felt the temperature outside that day. That’s when the hot sun feels best. Perhaps one of the few times when I can ignore the judgemental stares of passersby. Instead, I turn my palms up and let the warmth wash over me. It starts on my head, and I can feel the rays push past the strands and into my cerebrum. The light melts down my face, pulling my eyelids with it and casting a familiar fatigue over me. I imagine I look like a bloodhound asking the man in the sky for some help. The heat flows into my fingertips and erases anything extracorporeal for the duration of my sunbath. When I am 9, visiting family friends at their neighborhood pool, we lay tummy down on the cement after a swim. The summer sun has preheated our concrete cot. When I pull myself out of the freezing water in Penobscot Bay and regain feeling, it is the sun that washes over me. It heats me from every direction until I feel like the origin myself. At every age and in every place, I love the same warm sun.
