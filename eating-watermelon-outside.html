Descriptions of Things I Love the Most

Eating Watermelon Outside

I love eating watermelon outside with my shirt off. Usually I don’t like eating messy food. I don’t like worrying about barbecue sauce on my pants or a leak on my cheek. I don’t like when a too-tall burger oozes ingredients and expects me to unlock my jaw like a python. But when my toes touch the grass, I’m a whole different person. Matter of fact, toss me any fruit, and I will gnaw it down to its bones. Watch the juice drip down my chest as I toss a rind back to its birthplace. Forget cubes, I want the whole orb and someone to pass me triangles till my belly is an upstart farm. I like the detachment involved in this raw ripe ritual. My cuffs are not predators whose prey lies in the sauce cup, destined to dive in. My body is just my body, and a spill is just a chance to use the hose as a summertime shower. The stickiness is impermanent, and I can cleanse my soul of the sin of gluttony with the twist of a knob. I love eating watermelon outside in the grass with my shirt and shoes off during the summer.
