Descriptions of Things I Love the Most

Driving on a Summer Night

Driving with the windows down on a summer night is the closest us humans get to the unfiltered joy of a dog. Something about the way the air interacts with our skin. Warm and refreshing all at once. Sometimes I blast the music, and sometimes I like the silence and whatever ancillary noises the universe sneaks into my car. Sometimes it's better with no destination. In a hometown or a new city. I like the windows down all the way, giving the breeze unrestricted access to me and vice versa. I like it when I get the tail end of the sun, already below the horizon but still giving me light from its hiding spot. Or I like when it's late. Late enough that the sun’s sister is high in the sky and you wonder what the person in the occasional adjacent car is doing out. I like to take direction from our furry friends and get my head out the window. Let the elements wash over me, fill my ears with wind and my eyes with tears. Even better if there is a sunroof to reach my hand through and grab the universe. My thoughts might be sad or happy, confusing or melancholic but they will be big. I am lively and bright. Everything is light when I’m driving with the windows down on a summer night.
