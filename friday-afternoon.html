Descriptions of Things I Love the Most

Friday Afternoon

What’s better than a Friday afternoon? Nothing, since I was old enough to know the days of the week and their significance. Sunday evening is years away. Even the unlimited bounds of the seven-year-old mind could not conjure up a way to actualize the sometimes seventh day of the week. Thinking about Monday morning on a Friday is like thinking about taxes inside of your mother’s womb. No, Friday afternoon is free from that. Seventh grade, stepping off the bus is like discovering a new world. The air is open, and the freedom is palpable. There is a smell when I enter the house that is clean but unchemical, as if the heavy memory of the week has been erased from the surface of everything. In high school, the parking lot is consumed by an inextinguishable buzz. Reminiscent of insects excited by everything and nothing, the chatter centers on parties and plans and even illicit substances, but still the energy is innocent. At 25, it's a city that's brimming with the same feeling. This time, the transfer is more subtle and perhaps inaudible. It bounces between the intern, the analyst, and the banker across the street. The steps are lighter, and the man who wears a permanent frown lets it soften. Sure, it is commercialized and constructed but it is also connected. Running through all my years and all my peers is the sweet relief when we are released into the uncertain evening.
