Descriptions of Things I Love the Most

Taking Off My Ski Boots

It is a little bit of effort. Unbuckling. Shimmying and shaking. Sitting down and perhaps requiring an angle adjustment. But then it's smooth sailing. My ski boots slide and pop right off. The pressure valve releases. My toes wiggle, testing the unknown boundaries of freedom. They’ve been wrapped up in a straitjacket, buried alive and forgotten since the first run. Slowly, they realize they can separate and reacclimate to life outside the boot. It has been a hard day’s work for everyone. My feet are cold and weathered, but they are on the mend. Slipping them into a pair of unstructured canvas shoes might feel even better than the initial de-booting. My feet do not lack for memory and here comparison is not the thief of joy. Instead, they are gluttons for the mountain of satisfaction they get thinking back on their constrained past life, laughing from the comfort of their spacious new digs and looking forward to the prospect of warmth at the fingertips of a flame.
